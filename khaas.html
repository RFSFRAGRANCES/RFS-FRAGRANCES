When Performance May Be Lower

For the best lasting and projection, apply on clean, completely dry skin. Performance may feel weaker in these situations:

  • Heavy sweating: sweat and frequent wiping can disturb the fragrance on skin.
  • Very hot or humid weather: heat, humidity and outdoor activity can change how the scent projects and develops.
  • Very dry skin: fragrance may seem to fade faster on skin that is not moisturised.
  • Application on damp skin or too few sprays: water, sweat, rubbing the wrists, or applying less than the suggested amount can affect the result.

Tip: moisturise with an unscented lotion, let it absorb, then spray onto dry pulse points. Do not apply to irritated skin or rub the fragrance after spraying. Individual experience can still vary.
